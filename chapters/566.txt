Chapter 239: A Man Should Bleed on the Battlefield, Sword in hand, drunk with glory beneath the blazing sun!

Tu Ming was no ordinary figure—he wielded divine abilities and, among Nascent Soul cultivators, was honored with the title of True Lord.

He also held immense authority, being the High Priest of the Hundred Venoms Tribe. Among the great tribal forces of Thousand Peak Forest, the Azure Moon Tribe, Ancient Vine Tribe, and Hundred Venoms Tribe were the three most powerful, each with populations in the millions.

Yet despite his lofty status, Tu Ming’s contributions to the war in Thousand Peak Forest were meager. His achievements were pitiful, with his only notable performance being during the initial ambush battles.

He felt extremely stifled. If it weren’t for the terrifying suppression from Shen Qinghe, he would have undoubtedly gained more victories.

But reality was cruel—Tu Ming now teetered on the brink of death, placing all his hope in reinforcements.

Alas, the Hundred Venoms Tribe was comprised almost entirely of poison cultivators, who were like helpless chicks before Shen Qinghe’s wrath. Hadn’t the vast Poison Pool Mountain been wiped clean by her blade?

Shen Qinghe harbored a deep-seated hatred for all poison cultivators in the world. She had long sworn to hunt down Tu Ming. At this moment, she was combing the mountain inch by inch, leaving no path of escape.

As the glimmer of her azure spiritual light drew closer, about to fall upon him, Tu Ming sank into utter despair.

But just then, Shen Qinghe suddenly halted.

A flying message had reached her hands—within it, news of a sudden change on the frontlines.

“What? Commander Du has been wounded again by Young Master Shengxu? His injuries are so severe they must retreat?”

Shen Qinghe’s first reaction was disbelief. She suspected a ploy—a ruse by Tu Ming and his allies to deceive her into retreating.

Yet the national seals embedded within the letter radiated the unmistakable energy of the Two Note Kingdom—authentic and nearly impossible to forge.

“Seems this Tu Ming fellow’s luck hasn’t run out yet,” Shen Qinghe thought bitterly. A streak of cyan light flashed, and she took flight, swiftly withdrawing from Poison Pool Mountain.

Her ability to move so deep into Thousand Peak Forest had only been made possible because the main armies of Two Note Kingdom held firm in the core territory, granting her freedom to maneuver.

But now that the army was withdrawing step by step, the reaction speed of the Thousand Peaks Thousand Peaks Forest Coalition was unpredictable. If they regrouped and counterattacked, Shen Qinghe and others like her would be stranded deep behind enemy lines—an extremely dangerous situation.

To avoid encirclement, she had no choice but to retreat.

The defeated forces of the Thousand Peaks Forest Coalition were regrouping in one place.

“Lord Long… wuwuwu…” Many soldiers wept openly, grief and rage entwined in their cries.

The death of their commander had plunged the remnants of the army into a deep, desolate sorrow.

It wasn’t just Lord Long. The Dragon King Temple faction, the main force in this battle, had suffered the heaviest casualties.

Chen Lingfeng was being carried on a stretcher, unconscious. His body convulsed violently from time to time, white froth at the corners of his mouth, eyes rolled back and wide open—his eyelids refusing to close no matter how they tried.

This was the price of overtaxing one’s innate talent.

The latest reports had reached Lu Hongtu. At first, he couldn’t believe it. Only after verifying the contents did he cry out and spread the news to the rest of the army.

The soldiers’ faces remained blank at first.

But slowly, cheers began to rise—sporadic and hesitant.

Some cultivators proposed taking the opportunity to counterattack, but few responded.

Most shot down the idea, arguing that even though Du Tiechuan was gravely wounded, he wasn’t dead. Returning to battle would mean facing the powerful armies of the Two Note Kingdom once again. What difference would that make?

The difference… is that Lord Long is dead!

Who could now hold the line?

Lu Hongtu observed all this and shook his head silently:

“Morale like this is useless.”

Even with Shengxu’s victory and Du Tiechuan’s retreat, it couldn’t reverse the low spirits of the Thousand Peaks Forest Coalition. Most were now inclined toward rest and ceasefire rather than continuing the war.

“Unless I step forward and forcibly rally a new army. Or maybe Xiang Yue could do it…”

“But the influence we two wield only covers a portion. Compared to Lord Long? Not even close.”

“Forget it.”

“Truth be told, this outcome suits me just fine.”

Lu Hongtu was more than pleased with how things had turned out.

He had racked up significant merits in this campaign, spreading his name far and wide, forging alliances and good will with various cultivators and powers.

Diplomatically, both he and the Six Caverns Sect had made massive gains in the Thousand Peak Forest.

In terms of spoils, Lu Hongtu had collected a fair share as well.

All in all, the benefits far outweighed the losses.

The greatest loss the Six Caverns Sect suffered came from the Demon Heart Cavern’s side. Its young master, Lin You, had died in battle, and the cavern master himself had barely escaped with only his Nascent Soul.

But the Bloodshadow Cavern Master and Scorpion Venom Cavern Master—both handpicked by Lu Hongtu—had fought on the frontlines and survived, proving their worth.

“The Two Note Kingdom’s deployment of two Soul Formation-level powerhouses ended with one dead and one gravely wounded. They won’t be moving again anytime soon.”

The Ginseng Dragon King chose to strike at the Old Stone Monster, his hostility now laid bare. Though it wasn’t truly the Old Stone Monster himself, in essence, the Thousand Peaks Forest had already fallen into inner chaos.

“The upper echelons have suffered heavy losses and are locked in standoffs, each minding their own crisis. Isn’t this the perfect moment for my Six Caverns Sect to expand boldly and rise anew?”

Lu Hongtu was assessing the major powers within the Thousand Peaks Forest.

The largest faction, the Dragon King Temple, had taken the most devastating blow. Elder Long had always been the public face of the temple, and his death was an enormous loss.

Among Elder Long’s most trusted lieutenants, Wu Hen had already been captured alive. Chen Lingfeng, though he survived, was so mentally disordered that he caused immense trouble and losses to his own forces. He had lost all trust, made many enemies, and his reputation plummeted.

In such circumstances, Elder Long’s political legacy found no inheritor within his own faction. Strangely, it shifted to an outsider—Xiang Yue!

Thus, Lu Hongtu surmised, “Xiang Yue inherited Elder Long’s legacy, and his personal strength surged. His performance during the second great battle earned him widespread respect and recognition. He’ll become the representative of the beast cultivators.”

“As for me, I will be the leading figure among the human cultivators in the Thousand Peaks Forest.”

“The barbarian tribes have suffered greatly!”

“The Greenvine Healer of the Ancient Vine Tribe has fallen, though her Nascent Soul escaped. Die Lu of the Pale Moon Tribe died earlier, not even his Nascent Soul survived. Only Lone Fang is left supporting the situation. And as for the Hundred Poisons Tribe…”

“Who knows whether Tu Ming is dead? If he perished at the hands of Shen Qinghe, then the Hundred Poisons Tribe is bound to decline completely and will be forced to relinquish much of its former territory.”

“Even if he’s alive, based on his constant defeats during this campaign, his reputation has already hit rock bottom.”

The more Lu Hongtu thought, the more his heart burned with excitement.

The old powers that had once occupied and saturated many territories had all seen severe declines in strength.

The Six Caverns Sect and the Pale Barbarian Cultivators had both benefited from the war—they were destined to rise!

“Strategist Lu,” a divine sense whispered to him, “What can I gain if I join your sect?”

It was Li Jiuqu!

This gu cultivator had been suppressed in the battle by Mo Yechen’s eighth brother, losing many of his gu insects. Both his strength and reputation had taken a serious hit.

Now, seeing the upward trend of the Six Caverns Sect, he had taken the initiative to join.

“With a demonic powerhouse like this joining us, the strength of my Six Caverns Sect will soar!” Lu Hongtu couldn’t help but be elated.

He hadn’t even begun his grand campaign yet, and already he’d received such a promising opening move.

Truly marvelous!

At the same time, elsewhere…

Nascent Soul True Lord Bai Ling leaned against a pine tree, gazing at the cultivators around him.

They were Ju Bing, Qianye, and Jin Tunhe.

Bai Ling slowly opened his mouth: “The reason I’ve gathered you is simple—we’re all beast cultivators.”

Bai Ling himself was born of a white crane, Ju Bing was a mountain boar, Qianye a fox, and Jin Tunhe a toad.

Ju Bing and Qianye focused intently on Bai Ling, while Jin Tunhe yawned, eyes half-closed. He’d eaten far too much during the battle and now needed to rest and digest.

Bai Ling glanced at Jin Tunhe and said, “I’ll be brief. Although the Two Note Kingdom have retreated, they’ve preserved most of their strength.”

“Our side has now been defeated twice on the frontlines. The most recent one caused severe losses.”

“You and I are beast cultivators. We value freedom and solitude. But when the Two Note Kingdom resume hostilities, will the Thousand Peaks Forest still have such luck?”

Ju Bing asked directly, “So, Lord Bai Ling, what do you propose?”

Bai Ling smiled faintly. “Simple—we must join forces and look out for each other.”

Qianye said, “Should we contact the Elephant King? He’s a beast cultivator too.”

Bai Ling paused for a moment. “Xiang Yue is currently missing. But based on his strength, unless he encountered a Soul Transformation powerhouse, he should still be alive. Let’s discuss among ourselves first, then reach out to him.”

RUMBLE RUMBLE RUMBLE…

Xiang Yue thundered forward in pursuit of Ning Zhuo. Each of his steps caused the earth to quake, sending rocks flying and dust surging.

He sent a divine message: “Ning Zhuo, you brat, how much farther do you plan to run? Stop right there and explain everything to me. If you dare play games, I’ll tear you in half!”

Ning Zhuo responded, “Elephant King, don’t be hasty. We’re still too close to the battlefield. A bit farther would be safer, more secure.”

But Xiang Yue had run out of patience. “You’ve said that several times now. Do you take me for a fool? Halt at once!”

As he spoke, Xiang Yue suddenly struck out, martial power surging, his speed exploding. In a few quick steps, he closed the gap to Ning Zhuo.

He reached out a massive hand, aiming for the back of Ning Zhuo’s neck. If he caught him, Ning Zhuo would be hoisted like a helpless chick.

At the critical moment, a voice rang out:

“Elephant King, calm yourself.”

Bang.

Zhu Xuanji’s figure suddenly appeared, clashing head-on with Xiang Yue in a fierce exchange.

Both of them staggered back a step, forming a tense standoff.

Xiang Yue looked at Zhu Xuanji in astonishment. “A mere Golden Core, and you can match me evenly? Who are you?!”

Zhu Xuanji’s body shimmered as several divine arts quietly receded. He shook his numb fist and answered, “I am a royal of the Southern Bean Kingdom—Zhu Xuanji.”

Xiang Yue mulled it over. “A Divine Constable from Southern Bean?”

Clenching his fists, he declared, “Again!”

Muscles tensing and rippling, Xiang Yue unleashed a martial technique. His fists ignited with blood-flames as he struck once more.

Unshaken, Zhu Xuanji met him with calm resolve. Divine arts surged, golden light cloaked him, and jade-like energy unfurled around him. In that golden aura, mountain forests faintly emerged—every tree resplendent with golden branches and jade leaves, luxurious and noble.

Bang.

The two collided once again, both retreating at the same time—still evenly matched.

Xiang Yue’s expression grew solemn. Zhu Xuanji had matched him using only physical strength and divine arts—no treasures, no kingdom relics.

“I didn’t use my full power… but I was close.”

“And yet, both times, Zhu Xuanji and I were forced back a step. What does that mean?”

“It means—he didn’t use his full strength either. He merely gauged my power and responded with an equal measure!”

Realizing this, Xiang Yue let out a low murmur. “Impressive.”

He relaxed his fists, arms falling to his sides, and gave Zhu Xuanji a look of approval. “Was my meeting with Red Maple arranged by your Southern Bean Kingdom?”

Zhu Xuanji replied, “Elephant King, it was all a coincidence. Our kingdom simply went with the flow and played a minor piece.”

Xiang Yue’s heart sank. Zhu Xuanji’s candidness forced him to reevaluate the woman he had long cherished.

“Red Maple…” Xiang Yue was a man of deep affection—and Red Maple now carried his child.

The Pale Wild Elephant tribe faced great difficulty in reproduction. For the sake of his people’s survival, Xiang Yue had sought out Elephant Vein Fertility Pills multiple times.

And in this world, the higher one’s cultivation, the harder it became to bear children.

As the Elephant King, Xiang Yue bore both burdens. The odds of fathering a child were infinitesimal. There was no way he could ignore his lover—or his child!

“Sigh…” Xiang Yue let out a deep breath, then shot a look at Ning Zhuo before fixing his gaze on Zhu Xuanji. “What does your Southern Bean Kingdom want from me?”

“Hold on,” Ning Zhuo suddenly raised a hand. “I’ll excuse myself. You two can talk freely.”

Though he had close ties with Zhu Xuanji—and the royal Zhu clan had a longstanding alliance with the Ning family—Ning Zhuo knew his boundaries well.

Zhu Xuanji was representing the Southern Bean Kingdom and making arrangements in the Thousand Peak Forest. That was state-level secrecy. Ning Zhuo had no intention of prying too deeply.

But Zhu Xuanji simply said, “No need.”

Still, Ning Zhuo chuckled softly and chose to leave anyway.

Zhu Xuanji offered a calm and confident smile. “Ning Zhuo, if you don’t want to hear it, that’s fine. But don’t rush off— I have news about your mother. And this intel is of great value to you.”

Ning Zhuo froze. A beat later, his face changed dramatically, eyes brimming with emotion. “Lord Zhu, are you serious?”

Now it was Xiang Yue’s turn to chuckle. “So this is how the Divine Constables of Southern Bean do business?”

He had been caught by his weak spot and had no choice but to play along. But seeing Ning Zhuo caught the same way made him feel less resentful—and he even took the opportunity to mock Zhu Xuanji a little.

Ning Zhuo, however, bore no ill will toward Zhu Xuanji. In fact, he was moved. “Old Zhu is one of the few who knows my true purpose. I didn’t expect him to secretly gather intel for me, to do all this behind the scenes. Truly, he’s a good man.”

Nonetheless, he still stepped away, leaving Zhu Xuanji and Xiang Yue to speak alone.

He stopped halfway up the mountain, gazing at the distant peaks.

The setting sun bathed the sky in blood-red light.

This grand battle had raged since morning and only now was coming to an end.

Just then, a strong mountain wind rose, surging like a tide through the pine forests, whipping Ning Zhuo’s long hair and robes into a wild dance.

Sunset glow bathed his figure.

His white robes, now bloodstained, told of a fierce battle just passed. His blood still boiled, eyes shone bright, spirit blazing.

Memories of the battlefield surged within him—each clash, each life-or-death struggle, every hero showing their brilliance like actors on a stage.
The Mechanical Princess standing firm as the pillar of defense… Mulan’s godly arrows raining terror… Xu Dali’s relentless struggle… Zhao Xi’s sly escapes… Elder Long sacrificing himself … Xiang Yue crushing all in his path… Li Jiuqi’s Maggots and his sinister schemes… Chen Lingfeng falling to madness… Jin Tunhe’s unmatched talent… Lu Hongtu’s calm and steadiness… Ding Pi and Jia Wan brimming with murderous intent… Du Tiechuan’s divine power… Young Master Shengxu overturning the tide…

The Firecloud Battalion burned through countless foes… the White Jade Battalion was utterly annihilated… the Three Generals Battalion grew increasingly seasoned… the Red Flower Battalion reaped honor after honor…

All these moments flashed through his mind like galloping horses, stirring his emotions, until he let out a soft, exhilarated howl.

The youthful cry echoed through the mountains, stirring another gust of wind that swept upward like a rising sea.

“Without journeying, how can one truly taste such a magnificent life?”

“This kind of battlefield… I want to experience it again.”

“To clash with all kinds of great heroes, to bloom with everything you’ve got in a life-or-death struggle, to witness the brilliance of others while pushing yourself to your limits—it’s like drinking a strong liquor. You can’t help but become drunk with it.”

“Boss, I think I finally understand you.”

Ning Zhuo sent a mental transmission to Sun Lingtong: “Is this the thrill you’ve been chasing?”

Sun Lingtong’s inspiration flared, eyes sparkling. “This line says it all: ‘A man should shed blood on the battlefield, sword in hand, drunk with glory beneath the blazing sun.’”

Ning Zhuo chuckled. “A rare burst of poetry from the boss.”

Sun Lingtong giggled. “That’s the only line I’ve got.”

Ning Zhuo replied, “Then let me complete it for you.”

He thought for just three breaths before reciting:

“Divine arrows tear clouds, fires light the dusk,

Mad blades drink blood, iron blossoms bloom.

Thunderous ranks crush mountain peaks,

One cry sends wind sweeping through the sands.

A hundred battles, never fearing to turn to ash,

A thousand tribulations, yet spirit still boundless.

A man should shed blood on the battlefield,

Sword in hand, drunk with glory beneath the blazing sun!”

As his voice faded, Zhu Xuanji appeared and praised, “Excellent poem!”
